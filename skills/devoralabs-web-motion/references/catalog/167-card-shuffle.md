**Category:** Paper · **Kind:** effect

![Card shuffle demo](../../assets/gifs/167-card-shuffle.gif)

## Implementation recipe

Temporarily lift and reorder visual cards, then settle into a stack.

Restrict rotations/folds to artwork; real paragraphs, forms and controls remain upright.

**Starting timing:** start around 850ms for a discrete transition; ongoing indicators follow real activity and stop when it ends. The 3-second teaching GIF is not a required production duration.

## Adaptation checks

End in the intended readable, interactive state. Do not retain a clipped, distorted or transparent final control.

Preserve focus, selected items and form/demo state. Respect reduced motion and stop work in hidden views.

Verify a real intermediate frame and the settled state; do not infer visible movement from a class or animation object alone.
